# 02 · The Seven Continents of Xos

Xos has **seven continents**, holding ~29% of the surface as land. They are arranged so
that — like Earth — most land sits in the northern hemisphere, civilizations cluster
around temperate seas, and a few great frontiers (jungle, steppe, ice) stay wild far
into recorded history.

Coordinates below are **design-level** (latitude band + hemisphere), precise enough to
drive climate and place cultures, loose enough to stay a sandbox. Each continent lists
the **regions** the sim will subdivide it into (province-sized map cells).

## 1. Aurelia — *the temperate cradle*

- **Hemisphere / band:** Northern, temperate (35–58°N).
- **Climate:** Four strong seasons; mild Atlantic-style west coast, continental interior.
- **Geography:** A broad peninsula-rich landmass around the **Glass Sea**, backed by the
  **Caldemar Mountains** to the east. Long navigable rivers, fertile plains, an indented
  southern coastline studded with natural harbors.
- **Biomes:** Deciduous forest, river valleys, coastal Mediterranean fringe in the south.
- **Key resources:** Grain, timber, iron, horses, wine in the south.
- **Why it matters:** The **densest, most advanced civilizations start here** (see
  `03`). Aurelia is Xos's Mediterranean-Europe: the engine room of early history.
- **Regions (8):** Auria, Caldemar Reach, Glasswater, Norvane, Sundering Coast, Veld
  Marches, Old Aurel, Tarn Highlands.

## 2. Kessara — *the southern shore of empires*

- **Hemisphere / band:** Northern, subtropical (20–35°N), south of Aurelia across the Glass Sea.
- **Climate:** Mediterranean coast giving way to **desert and steppe** in the interior
  (great northern rain shadow + subtropical high).
- **Geography:** A long fertile coastal belt, the mighty **River Oksus** delta, and the
  **Sahel-like Khorr Steppe** rolling into deep desert southward.
- **Biomes:** Coastal farmland, oasis chains, desert, dry grassland.
- **Key resources:** Grain (delta), gold, salt, spices via caravan, fine horses (steppe).
- **Why it matters:** Cradle of the **oldest cities and writing**; a crossroads of sea
  trade (north) and caravan trade (south). Xos's Egypt-meets-Levant-meets-Sahara.
- **Regions (7):** Oksus Delta, Kessar, Khorr Steppe, The Reach of Salt, Sunspire Coast,
  Deep Khorr (desert), Caravan Gates.

## 3. Murunga — *the green frontier*

- **Hemisphere / band:** Western, equatorial-to-tropical (15°N–20°S).
- **Climate:** Savanna belts north and south, dense **equatorial rainforest** at the core,
  monsoon coasts.
- **Geography:** The **Murunga Basin** (a vast river system like the Congo/Amazon),
  highland savanna rims, the **Tooth Coast** of mangroves and reefs.
- **Biomes:** Rainforest, savanna, wetland, highland grassland.
- **Key resources:** Ivory, hardwoods, gold, copper, immense agricultural potential
  (under-exploited at 60 BC).
- **Why it matters:** Population-rich but **politically fragmented** at the start — a slow
  giant. Disease load (jungle) historically slowed conquest; the sim models this.
- **Regions (7):** Murunga Basin, Tooth Coast, Sava North, Sava South, The Highveld,
  Mangrove Marches, Deep Green.

## 4. Voskar — *the iron north*

- **Hemisphere / band:** Northern, boreal-to-temperate (45–70°N), east across the Auran Ocean.
- **Climate:** Cold continental; long winters, short fierce summers; taiga and tundra
  in the north.
- **Geography:** Endless **boreal forest**, the frozen **Vorn Lakes**, the **Skel
  Mountains**, a rugged fjorded north coast.
- **Biomes:** Taiga, tundra, cold steppe, temperate forest in the warmer south.
- **Key resources:** Furs, amber, iron, timber, whale and walrus (coast).
- **Why it matters:** Home to **hardy seafaring and horse cultures** — raiders and
  traders who pressure the wealthy south. Xos's Scandinavia-meets-Siberia. A reservoir
  of migration that can topple settled empires.
- **Regions (7):** Skelgard, Vorn Lakes, The Fjordlands, Ambercoast, Wolfsteppe, Deep
  Taiga, Frostmarch.

## 5. Zhang-Lu — *the river kingdoms*

- **Hemisphere / band:** Eastern, subtropical-to-temperate (25–45°N), south of Voskar.
- **Climate:** Monsoon-fed, humid subtropical south, temperate north; reliable rains.
- **Geography:** Dominated by **two great rivers** (the **Jin** and the **Lo**) and their
  flood plains — the most fertile land on Xos. Mountain walls to the west (the **Tian
  Wall**) seal it from the steppe.
- **Biomes:** River floodplain, terraced hills, subtropical forest, western highlands.
- **Key resources:** Rice and millet (huge surplus), silk, jade, tea, iron, ceramics.
- **Why it matters:** The **most populous and agriculturally dense** region on Xos. A
  cradle of a centralizing, bureaucratic civilization. Xos's China — a self-contained
  world-power.
- **Regions (8):** Jin Valley, Lo Valley, Tian Wall, The Terraces, Eastreach, Southmonsoon,
  Jade Hills, Greater Zhang.

## 6. Itzalan — *the highland sun*

- **Hemisphere / band:** Eastern/southern, tropical highland (5°N–25°S), across the Sunder.
- **Climate:** Tropical lowlands, but defined by a great **highland spine** (the **Q'an
  Cordillera**) giving cool, temperate "islands in the sky."
- **Geography:** Coastal rainforest, the high **Altun Plateau**, deep volcanic valleys,
  a dramatic Pacific-style west coast.
- **Biomes:** Rainforest, cloud forest, high plateau (alpine grassland), volcanic soils.
- **Key resources:** Maize-analog staple grains, cacao-analog, obsidian, gold, silver,
  llama-analog pack animals (highland).
- **Why it matters:** Cradle of **independent, isolated high civilization** — pyramids,
  astronomy, road networks — developing apart from the western/northern world. Xos's
  Andes-meets-Mesoamerica. First contact with it is a future flashpoint.
- **Regions (7):** Altun Plateau, Q'an Cordillera, Sunder Coast, Cloudvale, Obsidian
  Valleys, Lowland Itzal, The Far Terraces.

## 7. Thulven — *the last frontier*

- **Hemisphere / band:** Far southern, sub-antarctic (50–75°S).
- **Climate:** Cold, wet, storm-battered; tundra and cold rainforest; ice in the deep south.
- **Geography:** A wild continent of fjords, glaciers, peat moors, and the **Vorth Deep**
  storms on every shore.
- **Biomes:** Tundra, cold rainforest, glacier, moorland.
- **Key resources:** Whales, seals, fish, furs, hardy root crops — and rumored mineral
  wealth.
- **Why it matters:** **Largely unsettled at 60 BC** — scattered hunter-fisher peoples
  only. Thulven is Xos's wild card: a continent left for the sim's later centuries to
  discover, colonize, and fight over. Deliberately empty so history has somewhere to go.
- **Regions (5):** Vorthshore, The Moors, Glacier Coast, Peat Hollows, Far Thulven.
